## What Hoverday is

Hoverday compares forecast weather with the limits you set and shows airspace information copied from public FAA data. It gives information to help you plan drone flights. It does not decide whether a flight is legal or safe, and it does not grant any airspace authorization.

## Your responsibility

- You are the remote pilot in command and remain solely responsible for complying with all applicable laws and regulations (including 14 CFR Part 107 or 49 U.S.C. 44809 in the United States) and for the safe operation of your aircraft.
- Check the sky yourself before and during every flight, and check official sources for airspace and temporary flight restrictions.
- Do not use Hoverday as your only source of information.

## Data from other providers

Weather comes from Apple Weather and, in the United States, the National Weather Service; space weather from NOAA; airspace and TFR data from the FAA. These sources can be wrong, late, incomplete or unavailable, and Hoverday cannot correct them.
