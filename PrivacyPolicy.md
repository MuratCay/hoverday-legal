# Privacy Policy

Hoverday has no account and no sign-in, and it contains no advertising, analytics or tracking. This policy lists what leaves your iPhone and why.

## Location

Hoverday uses your location, while you use the app, to get the forecast and airspace for the spot where you fly. You can turn location off and search a place instead. Your location is not stored on any server of ours; Hoverday has no server.

## What is sent, and to whom

- Apple WeatherKit: your coordinates rounded to about 1 km, to get the weather forecast. No identifier is sent.
- National Weather Service (United States): the same rounded coordinates, when Apple Weather is unavailable.
- NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center: a request for the Kp index; no location is sent.
- FAA airspace and TFR services: the exact point you check, to find airspace and flight restrictions there.
- Apple MapKit: the text you type when you search a place.
- RevenueCat: an anonymous app user id and your App Store purchases, to unlock Premium. Nothing else is shared. Purchase attribution is turned off.

None of these requests is linked to your identity by Hoverday.

## Your records

Your flights, drones, batteries, certificates and saved spots stay on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your private iCloud database, which only you can read. Certificate photos and documents stay on this iPhone.

## Notifications

Reminders and alerts are scheduled on your iPhone; no push server is used.

## Children

Hoverday is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes and contact

This policy can change with an app update; the version in the app is always the current one. Questions about privacy: dev.murat.cay@gmail.com.
